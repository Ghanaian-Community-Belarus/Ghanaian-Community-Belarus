About Our Community

Who We Are

A non‑profit association of Ghanaians in Belarus. We foster unity, preserve our culture, and support integration and success for students, professionals, and families.

What We Do

  • Organize sports tournaments and viewing parties
  • Host cultural nights and end‑of‑year celebrations
  • Run language and settlement workshops
  • Coordinate with the Ghana Embassy on consular days

Belarus–Ghana Context

Belarus and Ghana are expanding cooperation in trade, education, and official travel, with new agreements and a joint trade committee. Many Ghanaian students choose Belarus for English‑medium programs and Russian language preparation.
