HEADLINE:

Ghana Community in Belarus Holds First Executive Meeting

Date: Auguest 25,2026 |Minsk,Belarus.

Article

The newly elected Executive of the Ghana Community in Belarus successfully held its first executive meeting yesterday, August 25, 2026, in Minsk.

The meeting was chaired by the President, Dr. Sampson Kofi Asamoah ,and was attended by all executive members. The purpose of the meeting was to set the tone for the new administration and to deliberate on strategic plans for the welfare and growth of the community.

Key issues discussed included:

  • Reaching out and Strengthening unity among Ghanaians in Belarus
  • Welfare and support systems for members
  • Community engagement and cultural activities
  • Collaboration with relevant authorities and stakeholders
  • Plans for upcoming community programs

The executive reaffirmed its commitment to transparent, inclusive, and service-oriented leadership.

The Ghana Community in Belarus appreciates the continued support of its members and looks forward to a productive year of unity and progress.
